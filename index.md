## Project 1
###Amazon Sales Report – Power BI Dashboard (Project Documentation)
Tool Used: Microsoft Power BI
Dataset: Amazon Orders / Sales Data
________________________________________
Project Objective
•	Analyze Amazon sales performance by quantity, revenue, profit, shipping cost, category, market, country, city, and ship mode.
•	Build an interactive dashboard for quick business insights and decision making.
________________________________________
Dashboard Components (Visual wise Explanation)
1. KPI Cards (Top Summary Section)
•	Sum of Quantity: Total number of items sold.
•	Sum of Sales: Total revenue generated.
•	Sum of Profit: Total profit earned.
•	Sum of Shipping Cost: Total logistics cost.
Purpose: Gives instant high level performance overview.
________________________________________
2. Category Slicer (Filter)
•	Filters data by Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology.
Purpose: Allows category wise sales and profit analysis.
________________________________________
3. Market Slicer (Filter)
•	Filters data by regions such as Africa, APAC, Canada, EMEA, etc.
Purpose: Enables region specific performance analysis.
________________________________________
4. Line / Area Chart – Sum of Sales by Ship Mode
•	Compares sales across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, Same Day shipping.
Purpose: Identifies the most preferred and revenue generating delivery method.
________________________________________
5. Bar Chart – Sum of Sales by Country
•	Displays top countries contributing to total sales (e.g., Egypt, Morocco, South Africa, etc.).
Purpose: Highlights high performing countries for market focus.
________________________________________
6. Bar Chart – Sum of Sales by City
•	Shows city level sales ranking.
Purpose: Helps identify major urban sales hubs.
________________________________________
7. Donut Chart – Sum of Sales by Category
•	Visualizes percentage contribution of each product category to total sales.
Purpose: Understands category dominance in revenue.
________________________________________
8. Donut Chart – Sum of Sales by Market
•	Shows revenue distribution across markets/regions.
Purpose: Evaluates regional business dependency and growth opportunities.
________________________________________
